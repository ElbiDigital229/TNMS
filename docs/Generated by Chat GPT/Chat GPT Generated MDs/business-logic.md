# TNMS – Business Logic

## Goal
Track accountability, not just completion.

## Core Idea
Delay is not failure — untracked delay is.

## Flow
Ticket → Assignment → Work → Blocker (if needed) → Resolution

## Insights
- Identify bottlenecks
- Department performance
- Manager accountability

## Outcome
CEO can see:
- Who is slow
- Why they are slow
- Which department is blocking
